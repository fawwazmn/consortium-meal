Approach

How I Work at Consortium Level

The principles I apply when MEAL has to work across several organizations.

Harmonize the minimum, not everything

Agree shared definitions, disaggregation and reporting calendars, and use partners' existing tools wherever they already meet the standard.

Quality before consolidation

Review and reconcile partner data before it is combined, so consortium figures can be traced back to their source.

Proportionate monitoring

Match process monitoring and PDM to the delivery modality, whether cash, voucher or in-kind, so evidence arrives in time to inform decisions.

Safe, minimal data

Report feedback trends without identifying people, and route sensitive and PSEA cases through safeguarding channels, not routine reporting.

Evidence that leads to action

Turn findings into short, decision-ready management information and narrative reporting that links results, challenges and adaptations.

Pooled-fund reporting

Familiar with the UHF operational framework, and applying my GIZ/BMZ, EU and US Government reporting experience to pooled-fund requirements.
